About Rewind Dynamics

Short: Rewind Dynamics is an defence-technology company developing autonomous systems for defence.

Long: Rewind Dynamics is a defence-technology company founded in 2020. It designs and develops autonomous systems — unmanned aerial, precision-guided and tactical platforms — for defence and military applications, built on a common core of autonomy, perception and dependable systems engineering. Its systems are under active research and development, with a first real-world mission test planned for December 2026.

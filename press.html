About Rewind Dynamics

Short: Rewind Dynamics is an Indian defence-technology company developing indigenous autonomous systems for defence.

Long: Rewind Dynamics is an Indian defence-technology company headquartered in New Delhi, founded in 2020. It designs and develops indigenous autonomous systems — unmanned aerial, humanoid and tactical miniature platforms — for defence and homeland-security applications, built on a common core of autonomy, perception and dependable systems engineering. Its systems are under active research and development, with a first real-world mission test planned for December 2026.

The Opportunity

Autonomy is the defining defence technology of the decade.

§ 01 / OPPORTUNITY

Around the world, defence is moving decisively toward autonomous and uncrewed systems. Every serious force is investing in the ability to sense, decide and act without a human in every loop — and demand is set to grow for years to come.

Yet trustworthy, software-first autonomy remains under-served. That is the gap Rewind Dynamics is built to fill: a focused team building the autonomy core that unmanned aerial, precision-guided and tactical platforms all depend on. Back the core, and you back every platform that runs on it.

Tailwind

Structural demand

Defence spending on autonomy and uncrewed systems is a durable, worldwide, multi-year tailwind.

Timing

Early, foundational

We're at the ground floor — building the core before the platforms scale around it.

Inflection

First mission test

Our first real-world mission test is planned for December 2026 — a meaningful milestone to back ahead of.

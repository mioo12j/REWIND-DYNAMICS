The Opportunity

Autonomy is the defining defence technology of the decade.

§ 01 / OPPORTUNITY

Around the world, defence is moving decisively toward autonomous and uncrewed systems. India — with a clear national mandate for self-reliance in defence — is set to be one of the most important markets of all, and it wants that capability built at home.

Yet indigenous, software-first autonomy remains under-served in India. That is the gap Rewind Dynamics is built to fill: a focused team building the autonomy core that unmanned aerial, humanoid and tactical platforms all depend on. Back the core, and you back every platform that runs on it.

Tailwind

National mandate

India's push for self-reliance in defence manufacturing is a durable, policy-level tailwind.

Timing

Early, foundational

We're at the ground floor — building the core before the platforms scale around it.

Inflection

First mission test

Our first real-world mission test is planned for December 2026 — a meaningful milestone to back ahead of.

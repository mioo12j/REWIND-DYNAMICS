Who We Are  ·  Est. 2020  ·  New Delhi

An Indian defence
technology company.

Rewind Dynamics was established in 2020 and is headquartered in New Delhi. We design and develop indigenous autonomous systems for defence and homeland security — built to be useful, dependable and worth trusting.

Our Story

We started Rewind Dynamics to build autonomy for defence, from India.

§ 01 / STORY

Autonomous systems are becoming important to defence around the world. We think India should have strong, home-grown capability in this area — and we started Rewind Dynamics to help build it.

Our focus is the autonomy itself: the software and engineering that let a machine sense its surroundings, make decisions and move on its own. We're applying that to autonomous bodies — unmanned aerial, humanoid and tactical platforms.

Our name reflects how we intend to work. We record each test, rewind it, understand what happened, and use that to make the next version better. Progress in autonomy comes from disciplined iteration.

We've been at this since 2020, building the foundations deliberately — the autonomy, the team and the engineering. Our priority has been getting the fundamentals right rather than rushing to announce finished products.

Mission

Build autonomy that keeps people safer.

To develop autonomous systems that can take on tasks which are dull, repetitive or dangerous — so that people can stay at a safer distance.

Vision

Strong Indian capability in defence autonomy.

To contribute to India's capability in autonomous systems, with technology that is dependable, accountable and made here.

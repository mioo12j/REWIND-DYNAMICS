Who We Are  ·  Est. 2020  ·  Global

A close-knit team
with a long horizon.

We started in 2020 with a small room and one conviction: that the forces who depend on autonomous systems should be able to understand them. Six years later we are still small, still stubborn about that, and building for governments and armed forces worldwide — with a first real-world mission test coming this December.

Our Story

Six years of foundations,
and one test ahead.

§ 01 / STORY

Autonomous systems are becoming important to defence around the world. We think the forces that rely on them deserve capability they can actually understand and own — and we started Rewind Dynamics to help build it.

Our focus is the autonomy itself: the software and engineering that let a machine sense its surroundings, make decisions and move on its own. We're applying that to autonomous bodies — unmanned aerial, precision-guided and tactical platforms.

Our name reflects how we intend to work. We record each test, rewind it, understand what happened, and use that to make the next version better. Progress in autonomy comes from disciplined iteration.

We've been at this since 2020, building the foundations deliberately — the autonomy, the team and the engineering. Our priority has been getting the fundamentals right rather than rushing to announce finished products.

Mission

Send the machine, not the person.

To develop autonomous systems that can take on tasks which are dull, repetitive or dangerous — so that people can stay at a safer distance.

Vision

Autonomy that earns the trust placed in it.

To become a dependable source of autonomous systems for defence and military users around the world — with technology that is dependable, accountable and owned end to end.

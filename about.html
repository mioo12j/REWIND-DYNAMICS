Who We Are

A defense company
built for what's next.

We're engineering the autonomy that will define the next era of national security — resilient, intelligent systems that keep people out of harm's way and give democracies the decisive edge.

Our Story

Founded on a simple conviction: autonomy is the new deterrent.

§ 01 / STORY

The character of conflict is changing. Speed, mass and machine intelligence increasingly decide outcomes — and the side that fields autonomy first, and fields it well, holds the advantage.

Rewind Dynamics was founded to make sure that advantage belongs to those who defend freedom. We're building the autonomy stack, perception intelligence and mission software that let autonomous bodies operate with real initiative — in the air, on the ground, on the water and beneath it.

We take our name from how we get better. Every mission our systems run is captured, replayed and understood — we rewind reality, learn from it, and push the improvement across the fleet. It's a company designed to compound: the more we operate, the sharper we become.

Today we're focused on the fundamentals — the hard, unglamorous engineering that resilient autonomy demands. No hype, no vaporware. Just the foundation that real platforms will be built on.

Mission

Keep people safe by putting machines forward.

To design and field autonomous systems that take on the dull, dirty and dangerous — extending human judgement into contested space while keeping operators out of it.

Vision

Autonomy that democracies can trust.

A world where the defenders of open societies out-pace any threat — because their machines sense faster, reason better and act with disciplined, accountable autonomy.

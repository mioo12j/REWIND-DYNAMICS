Autonomy Core

The brain of the platform. A closed-loop autonomy engine that plans, adapts and manoeuvres in real time — with no human in the loop and no dependence on the network.

  • Autonomous mission planningDynamic route, task and behavior planning that re-plans on the fly as the situation changes.
  • Contested-environment navNavigation and guidance resilient to GPS denial, jamming and deception.
  • Bounded, accountable behaviorAutonomy operating inside defined rules of engagement, fully auditable after the fact.

Common Questions

Straight answers.

Our foundation is the autonomy stack, perception intelligence and mission software — the intelligence layer that makes a body autonomous. It's engineered to be portable across platforms, and platform work follows as the stack matures.
Our systems execute their mission entirely onboard. If the communications link is jammed, degraded or absent, the platform keeps sensing, reasoning and acting. Connectivity improves the picture — it is never required to function.
Autonomy operates inside clearly defined bounds and rules of engagement, with humans on the loop. Every decision is recorded and auditable — machines act, humans stay responsible.
We're focused on the fundamentals of resilient autonomy rather than a single announced product. If you're a partner or program with a hard problem, get in touch.

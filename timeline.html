Company  ·  Timeline

2020 to 2026.

A short, honest record of how Rewind Dynamics has developed — the decisions that shaped the company, the programmes we opened, and the milestone still ahead of us. We have deliberately built foundations before announcing products.

How To Read It

Foundations first.

§ 01 / CONTEXT

Six years is a long time to work without a fielded product, and we are frequently asked about it. The answer is that autonomy rewards patience: the architecture decisions made early — running intelligence on the platform, keeping a human in command, proving behaviour in simulation before hardware — are extremely expensive to reverse later.

So the timeline below is mostly a record of foundations: a position taken, a team assembled, a programme opened, an architecture settled. The milestone that matters most is the one still ahead — our first real-world mission test, planned for December 2026.

Milestones

The record
so far.

§ 02 / MILESTONES
26 Jan 2020

Founded

Rewind Dynamics is established with a single objective: build autonomous systems that defence and military users can genuinely trust.

 Aug 2020

A position on trustworthy autonomy

We publish our view that the forces depending on autonomous systems should be able to inspect, adapt and own them.

 Mar 2021

Building the founding team

Recruitment begins in earnest across autonomy, perception, robotics and embedded systems.

 Sep 2021

Committing to the edge

We settle a foundational architectural decision: intelligence runs on the platform, never dependent on a link.

 May 2022

First unmanned aerial system programme

Focused development begins on our first autonomous UAV.

 Nov 2022

Human always in command

We formalise the principle that governs every system we build: machines execute, people decide.

 Feb 2024

Engineering facility expanded

More space, better test infrastructure and room to grow the team.

 Apr 2025

Precision guidance programme opens

A dedicated programme begins on navigation, perception and decision software for precision guidance.

 Jun 2026

The tactical edge thesis

We set out why small autonomous systems change what a dismounted team can see and do.

 Dec 2026

First real-world mission test

Planned: our first end-to-end mission test, moving from simulation into the physical world.

Dates reflect company milestones and published positions. Our systems remain in active research and development; nothing has been fielded. The December 2026 mission test is planned, not completed.

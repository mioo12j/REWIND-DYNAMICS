Our Systems

The systems
we build.

Rewind Dynamics develops three families of autonomous systems for defence and military applications — unmanned aerial, humanoid and tactical. Each shares a common core of autonomy, perception and dependable engineering; each is shaped for a different mission.

S-01

Unmanned Aerial

Autonomous aerial systems for surveillance, reconnaissance and force protection.

S-02

Humanoid

Human-form robotic platforms for roles that suit a legged, human-scale system.

S-03

Tactical Miniature

Compact, man-portable systems for close-range and dismounted use.

All three families are currently under active research and development — nothing has been fielded, and our first real-world mission test is planned for December 2026. We share the shape of what we're building here; detailed specifications are shared directly with partners, evaluators and the armed forces under appropriate terms — contact us to discuss.

System 01

Unmanned Aerial Systems

Aircraft that fly and complete a mission with minimal operator workload. We develop autonomous UAVs for intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance (ISR) and for force protection — systems that can watch, understand and report from positions we'd rather not put people in.

Our engineering emphasis is on autonomy that holds up in the field: reliable navigation, useful onboard perception, and graceful behaviour when conditions or communications turn difficult.

  • Autonomous flight & navigationPlan, fly and adjust a route with the operator supervising rather than piloting.
  • Onboard perceptionDetect and track what matters, and report it clearly.
  • Resilient by designSensible behaviour when the link is degraded or the picture is incomplete.

System 02

Humanoid Systems

Robots built in a human form, for roles where that shape is genuinely the right one — moving through spaces made for people, using equipment made for people, and operating where wheels and tracks cannot. This is a serious, long-horizon research programme for us.

Humanoid robotics is one of the hardest problems in the field. We're focused on the fundamentals that make it viable: locomotion, balance, and control that stay stable in the real world.

  • Locomotion & balanceWalking, recovering and staying upright over uneven ground.
  • Perception & navigationUnderstanding a space and moving through it purposefully.
  • Safe around peopleDesigned to operate predictably alongside human teams.

System 03

Tactical Miniature Systems

Small, man-portable autonomous systems for close-range and dismounted use. When size, weight and power are tightly limited, simplicity and ruggedness matter more than anything — and that constraint is exactly what we find interesting.

The goal is capable autonomy in a package a soldier can carry and trust: quick to deploy, light on logistics, and dependable when it counts.

  • Compact autonomyMeaningful capability on small, low-power hardware.
  • Rugged & simpleBuilt to be dependable and easy to operate under pressure.
  • Rapid to deployMinimal setup and a light logistics footprint.

Responsible By Design

People stay in command.

Our systems are designed so that a person supervises and can intervene. Machines carry out tasks; people set the rules and remain responsible. We treat safety, accountability and human oversight as design requirements, not afterthoughts.

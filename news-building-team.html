Building the founding engineering team

Great autonomy comes from great engineers. An update on the team we're assembling and the disciplines we're hiring for.

A company like ours is only as good as the people who build it. Over the past year our focus has been on assembling a founding engineering team with the range of skills that autonomous systems demand.

The disciplines it takes

Autonomy sits at the meeting point of several hard fields, and we're bringing them together under one roof:

  • Autonomy and planning — the decision-making core of every system.
  • Perception and computer vision — turning sensor data into understanding.
  • Robotics and controls — flight, locomotion and stable motion.
  • Embedded and edge systems — making it all run on real, constrained hardware.

How we work

We keep teams small, ownership high, and the loop between building and testing short. People here own outcomes rather than tickets, and the best idea wins regardless of who has it. We test honestly and let the data decide.

We're hiring builders — people who would rather make the thing than talk about it.

Join us

The people who join now will shape both the technology and the company. If that appeals to you, take a look at our open roles — or just write to us and tell us where you'd make the biggest difference.

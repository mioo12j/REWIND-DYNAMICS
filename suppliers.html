Company  ·  Suppliers

Suppliers and
supply chain.

We build the autonomy; we do not pretend to build everything else. Sensors, compute, propulsion, radios, structures and manufacturing all come from companies that do those things better than we could — and choosing them well is a genuine engineering decision, not a procurement formality.

Our Approach

Suppliers are
design partners.

§ 01 / APPROACH

In a size-, weight- and power-constrained system, component choices dictate what is architecturally possible. A sensor's characteristics change what perception can achieve; a compute module's thermal envelope changes what autonomy can run. We would rather involve suppliers early, as design partners, than issue a specification at the end.

We are a small company at an early stage, so we are direct about scale: our volumes today are development volumes. What we can offer instead is a serious technical relationship, clear requirements, and prompt, honest communication.

What We Look For

How we choose.

Beyond the obvious question of whether a component performs, these are the things that decide whether a supplier relationship works.

  • Technical honestyDatasheets that hold up in our conditions, and a straight answer when something will not do what we need.
  • Supply resilienceAvailability we can plan a programme around, and early warning of changes or obsolescence.
  • ComplianceWillingness and ability to work within applicable export-control and trade obligations.
  • Engineering accessSomeone technical we can actually talk to when an integration question arises.

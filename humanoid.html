System 02  ·  Humanoid

Humanoid
systems.

Robots built in a human form, for roles where that shape is genuinely the right one — moving through spaces made for people, using equipment made for people, and going where wheels and tracks cannot. This is a serious, long-horizon research programme.

Why Human Form

The world is already
built for people.

§ 01 / RATIONALE

A human-shaped robot is not a novelty — it is a way to inherit an entire world of infrastructure for free. Doors, stairs, ladders, vehicles, handles and tools were all designed around human proportions. A machine with roughly those proportions can use them without anyone rebuilding the environment.

We are deliberately honest about this: humanoid robotics is one of the hardest open problems in the field, and we treat it as research rather than a product roadmap. The payoff, if it works, is a platform that can go where a tracked robot cannot and do work that would otherwise put a person at risk.

The Programme

Fundamentals before features.

We are not chasing demonstrations. The programme is focused on the small number of things that decide whether a legged, human-scale platform is viable at all.

  • Locomotion & balanceWalking, recovering from a stumble and staying upright over ground that was never made flat.
  • Whole-body controlCoordinating many joints at once so the machine moves deliberately rather than reactively.
  • Perception & navigationUnderstanding a space — stairs, doorways, obstacles — and moving through it purposefully.
  • Safe around peoplePredictable behaviour and clear limits, because these platforms work alongside human teams.

Where It Could Matter

Roles worth
the difficulty.

We describe these as directions, not commitments. Each is a role where sending a machine instead of a person is the entire point.

§ 02 / ROLES
01

Hazardous entry

Going first into a structure or environment that is unsafe for people.

02

Human-built spaces

Stairs, ladders, doorways and vehicles that wheels and tracks simply cannot manage.

03

Heavy or repetitive work

Sustained physical tasks that wear people down over a long deployment.

04

Working beside a team

Operating predictably in the same space as the people it supports.

Humanoid robotics is our longest-horizon effort. This system is under active research and development. Nothing described here has been fielded, and we do not publish specifications or performance figures. Our first end-to-end mission test is planned for December 2026. Detail is shared directly with partners, evaluators and the armed forces under appropriate terms — get in touch.

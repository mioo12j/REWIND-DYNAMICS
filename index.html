The Company

Autonomy, built for the mission at hand.

§ 01 / OVERVIEW

Rewind Dynamics is a defence-technology company headquartered in New Delhi. We design and develop autonomous systems that can sense their surroundings, make decisions and act — reducing risk to personnel and extending what a single operator can achieve.

Our work spans three families of autonomous platforms — unmanned aerial, precision-guided and tactical — unified by a common core of autonomy, perception and dependable systems engineering. We build for real operating conditions, for defence and military users worldwide. We share what we're working on; the specifics we keep for the people who need them.

Why Autonomy, Why Now

Capability you can trust, not a black box.

Autonomy is becoming decisive across modern defence. We believe the forces that depend on these systems should be able to understand, adapt and improve them — not treat them as sealed imports.

Rewind Dynamics is being built in that spirit: to contribute trustworthy, software-first autonomous systems to defence and military users around the world, engineered from first principles and owned end to end.

Our Approach

The name is the method.

We record every test, rewind it, understand exactly what happened, and feed that back into the next iteration. Dependable autonomy is earned through disciplined engineering and honest testing — not a single demonstration.

It is a deliberately rigorous way to build. It keeps us honest about what we're actually building, and it is how we intend to earn trust — with operators, partners and the institutions we serve.

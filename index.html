Who We Are

A young defence technology company, focused on building autonomous systems.

§ 01 / COMPANY

Founded in 2024 and based in New Delhi, Rewind Dynamics is an early-stage team working on autonomy for defence — the software and engineering that lets a machine sense its surroundings, make decisions and move on its own.

We are at the build stage. Rather than announce finished products, we are concentrating on the fundamentals: autonomy, perception and the systems engineering behind unmanned aerial, humanoid and tactical platforms. This page is here so people know what we're working on and how to reach us.

Our Approach Test · Learn · Iterate
DesignStart from the problem and the mission need.
SimulateTest in software before anything is built.
TestTrial in the real world and record everything.
RewindReview the run, learn from it, and improve.

Why "Rewind"

Named for how we intend to work.

The name reflects a simple idea: record each test, rewind it, understand what happened, and use that to make the next version better. Good autonomy comes from disciplined iteration, not from a single big reveal.

We are early, and we would rather be honest about that than over-promise. The aim is steady progress on hard engineering — and to build systems we would trust ourselves.

How the technology works

Autonomy will be central to the next decade of defence technology.

We think autonomous systems will play a growing role in defence — not to replace people, but to handle tasks that are dull, repetitive or dangerous, and to keep operators at a safer distance. Rewind Dynamics is being built to contribute to that in India.

Who We Are

We build machines that think, move and decide — so the people who matter don't have to be where the danger is.

§ 01 / MISSION

Modern operations are defined by speed, uncertainty and contested space. Rewind Dynamics exists to close the gap between decision and action — delivering autonomous systems that sense, reason and manoeuvre at the tempo of the fight.

We are a new kind of defense company: software-defined, hardware-fluent, and built to iterate. Rather than delivering a single product, we develop the autonomy stack, perception intelligence and resilient mission software that lets autonomous bodies operate with initiative — even when communications, GPS and human oversight are degraded or denied. This is the foundation. The platforms follow.

How We Build

Named for how we get better — fast.

Every mission is recorded, replayed and understood. We rewind reality, learn from it, and roll the improvement out across the fleet. Software moves at the speed of software — and the hardware inherits the intelligence.

This is a company built to compound. The more our systems operate, the sharper they become — a flywheel of perception, autonomy and field-proven trust.

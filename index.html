The Company

Indigenous autonomy, built for the mission at hand.

§ 01 / OVERVIEW

Rewind Dynamics is an Indian defence-technology company headquartered in New Delhi. We design and develop autonomous systems that can sense their surroundings, make decisions and act — reducing risk to personnel and extending what a single operator can achieve.

Our work spans three families of autonomous platforms — unmanned aerial, humanoid and tactical — unified by a common core of autonomy, perception and dependable systems engineering. We build for real operating conditions, and we build in India, because sovereign capability in autonomy matters. We share what we're working on; the specifics we keep for the people who need them.

Why India, Why Now

Sovereign capability in autonomous systems.

Autonomy is becoming decisive across modern defence. We believe India should own this capability at home — designed, engineered and understood within the country, not imported as a black box.

Rewind Dynamics is being built in that spirit: to contribute indigenous, trustworthy autonomous systems to India's defence and homeland-security ecosystem, aligned with the national push for self-reliance in defence manufacturing.

Our Approach

The name is the method.

We record every test, rewind it, understand exactly what happened, and feed that back into the next iteration. Dependable autonomy is earned through disciplined engineering and honest testing — not a single demonstration.

It is a deliberately rigorous way to build. It keeps us honest about what we're actually building, and it is how we intend to earn trust — with operators, partners and the institutions we serve.

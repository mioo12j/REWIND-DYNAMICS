Core Competencies

What we
actually do.

Six competencies, one shared core. Improvements to the core reach every programme at once.

§ 02 / COMPETENCIES
01

Autonomy

Onboard planning, decision-making and bounded discretion — a system that pursues an objective within limits a person has set. Detail →

02

Perception & sensor fusion

Detection, tracking and multi-sensor fusion in clutter and poor conditions, with honest confidence attached. Detail →

03

GPS-denied navigation

Visual odometry, relocalisation and inertial dead reckoning fused so the system degrades honestly rather than drifting silently. Detail →

04

Edge intelligence

Capable inference inside a small size, weight and power budget, with deterministic real-time behaviour. Detail →

05

Precision guidance

Navigation, perception and decision software applied to the accuracy chain, under human authorisation. Detail →

06

Systems engineering & assurance

Integration, environmental ruggedness, repeatable verification and configuration control. Detail →

Differentiators

Why us,
specifically.

Four things that are true of us and not automatically true of everyone in this field.

§ 03 / DIFFERENTIATORS
01

One core, three families

We build the autonomy core rather than bespoke stacks, so an improvement made for one platform benefits all of them.

02

Designed to integrate

Modular by intent. Our software is built to sit alongside existing platforms, sensors and command arrangements — not to replace them.

03

Owned end to end

Engineered from first principles, inspectable and adaptable, rather than delivered as a sealed black box.

04

We state our stage

Nothing has been fielded and every page says so. You will always know exactly what is demonstrated and what is not.

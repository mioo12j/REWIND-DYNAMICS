Cookie Policy.

The short version: this site sets no cookies and tracks nothing. Here is exactly what happens when you visit, and who is involved. Last updated 29 August 2026.

No cookies, no tracking

Rewind Dynamics LLC does not set any cookies on this website. We use no advertising cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting and no cross-site profiling. There is nothing to accept or reject, which is why you will not see a cookie banner.

What actually happens when you visit

A few technical things are unavoidable for any website to work. None of them identify or profile you:

WhatWhyWhoCookie?
Server request logs (including your IP address, browser and time)Serving the pages and protecting the site from abuseCloudflare Pages (our host)No
Web font files are requested from Google's font serversTo render the site's typefacesGoogle Fonts — receives your IP address to deliver the fontsNo
Contact/careers form submissionOnly when you submit a form, to deliver it to our inboxFormSubmit (our form relay)No

The site does not use browser localStorage or sessionStorage to store anything about you either. Any motion effects run entirely in your browser and respect your "reduce motion" setting.

A note on Google Fonts

Because the fonts load from Google's servers, Google receives your IP address at the moment the fonts are fetched, purely to deliver the files. It is not a cookie and we do not receive that data. If you would prefer to avoid it entirely, most browsers and privacy tools let you block third-party requests.

If this changes

If we ever introduce analytics or any cookie, we will update this page and our Privacy Policy first and, where the law requires it (for example under EU/UK ePrivacy rules), ask for your consent before setting anything non-essential.

Questions

Email info@rewinddynamics.com or see our Privacy Policy for the full picture of how we handle information.

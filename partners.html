Partners & Programmes

Who we
work with.

Serious autonomy is not built alone. We work with defence programmes, evaluators, integrators, suppliers and research institutions — and we are deliberately open about how to start a conversation with us, wherever in the world you are.

Our Position

We build the core.
We integrate with the rest.

§ 01 / APPROACH

Rewind Dynamics is focused on the autonomy core — the software and engineering that let a platform sense, decide and act. We deliberately design that core to be modular, so it can sit alongside existing platforms, sensors and command arrangements rather than replacing them.

That makes us a natural partner rather than a competitor to most of the defence supply chain. If you build airframes, sensors, radios or ground systems, our work is meant to make yours more capable — not to lock anyone into a closed stack.

How We Work

Straightforward to deal with.

We are an R&D-stage company and we behave like one. That means being precise about what exists today, and what is still being built.

  • We don't over-claimNothing has been fielded yet. We will tell you exactly what stage a capability is at.
  • Detail goes privateWe publish the shape of our work, not specifications. Substance is shared directly, under appropriate terms.
  • Lawful and compliantWe work within applicable export-control and defence-trade rules, and we expect our partners to do the same.
  • Human command, alwaysOur systems are designed so a person sets the rules and stays responsible. That is not negotiable.

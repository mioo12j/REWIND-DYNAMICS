Insights

Perspectives on
defence autonomy.

Longer pieces on the questions we spend our time thinking about. These are arguments rather than announcements — our view of where autonomy is going, what is genuinely hard about it, and what we think the field gets wrong.

Why We Publish

Positions, openly held.

§ 01 / INTENT

Defence technology has a habit of being discussed either in marketing language or not at all. We think there is room for a third register: writing plainly about hard problems, including the parts we have not solved.

Nothing here contains specifications, performance figures or operational detail — that is shared privately, under appropriate terms. What we can do publicly is set out how we think. If you disagree with any of it, we would genuinely like to hear why.

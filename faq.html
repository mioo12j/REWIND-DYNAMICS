Straight answers about who we are, what we're building, where we are in the journey, and how to work with us.

Rewind Dynamics is an Indian defence-technology company, based in New Delhi, that designs and develops indigenous autonomous systems — unmanned aerial (UAV), humanoid and tactical miniature platforms — for defence and homeland-security applications.
Rewind Dynamics was founded on 26 January 2020 in New Delhi, India.
We are a research-and-development company. Our systems are under active development and have not been fielded or tested in the real world yet. Our first end-to-end mission test is planned for December 2026.
Three families that share a common autonomy core: unmanned aerial systems (UAVs) for surveillance and force protection; humanoid systems (a long-horizon research programme); and tactical miniature systems for close-range, dismounted use.
It means designed, engineered and understood within India, in line with the national push for self-reliance in defence. We believe sovereign capability in autonomy is capability you can trust, adapt and maintain on your own terms.
A person is always in command. Machines carry out tasks; people set the rules and stay responsible. We design for human oversight, safety limits, lawful use, accountability and honest after-action assessment. See our Responsible Autonomy page.

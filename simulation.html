Method  ·  Simulation & Test

Simulation
and test.

Most of our progress happens in software. Testing an idea in simulation is faster, safer and repeatable in a way that field testing never is — so we find problems on screen, and only move to hardware once the software holds up.

The Method

Prove it in software first.

§ 01 / APPROACH

Autonomy is developed by iteration, and iteration speed decides how good a system eventually becomes. In the field, a single test can take a day to set up and produce one noisy result. In simulation, the same scenario can be run thousands of times before lunch, with every variable controlled.

That does not make simulation a substitute for reality — it makes it the filter. By the time something reaches hardware, the obvious failures are gone, and the tests that remain are the ones that genuinely need the physical world.

The Loop

Test. Rewind.
Assess. Improve.

Our name describes the method. Every run — simulated or physical — is recorded, replayed and assessed honestly. What went right, what went wrong, and why.

  • SimulateRun the scenario in software, as many times and as many ways as needed.
  • Test on hardwareMove to real devices once the behaviour holds up in software.
  • RecordCapture everything, so a run can be reconstructed exactly rather than remembered.
  • Assess honestlyReview what actually happened without flattering the result, then feed it back in.

What We Test For

The cases that
actually matter.

Anything can be made to work in the nominal case. We spend our time on everything else.

§ 02 / SCENARIOS
01

Degraded sensing

Poor light, weather, occlusion, and sensors that partially fail.

02

Lost communications

What the platform does when the link thins, drops, or returns unexpectedly.

03

Unexpected world

Situations the system was not explicitly designed for, to see how it fails.

04

Edge of the envelope

Behaviour right at the boundary of the limits an operator has set.

05

Repeatability

Whether the same input reliably produces the same behaviour.

06

Hand-off

Transferring control between autonomy and operator without confusion.

Our systems have not been fielded. Everything to date has lived in development and simulation; our first end-to-end real-world mission test is planned for December 2026. We would rather say that plainly than imply more.

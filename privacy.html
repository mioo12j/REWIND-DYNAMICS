Privacy Policy.

What we collect through this website, how we use it, and the rights you have — under the EU/UK GDPR and California privacy law. We keep data collection to the minimum. Last updated 29 August 2026.

NOTE — This policy is written in good faith to describe our actual practices. It is not legal advice; please have qualified counsel review it for your jurisdiction before relying on it.

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Rewind Dynamics LLC ("Rewind Dynamics", "we", "us", "our"), a defence-technology company registered in New Mexico, United States. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right described below, email info@rewinddynamics.com. We are the data controller for the information described in this policy.

All complaints, notices and legal correspondence must be sent to us digitally, by email to info@rewinddynamics.com. We do not accept service of notices, complaints or legal process by post or other physical delivery — see section 13.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect what you choose to give us, plus the minimal technical data every website receives to function. We do not run advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or profiling.

  • Information you submit through our forms — your name, email address, phone number and organisation (where provided), the content of your message or job application, and the enquiry or role type you select.
  • Basic technical data — your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, which are handled by our hosting provider as part of serving and securing the site. We do not use this to identify or profile you.

Please do not submit sensitive, export-controlled or classified information through this website. If a matter is sensitive, tell us it exists and we will arrange an appropriate secure channel.

3. How & Why We Use It

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, evaluate job applications, and communicate with you about your request. We use technical data to operate, secure and troubleshoot the site. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR / UK GDPR:

  • Consent — when you voluntarily submit a form, for us to respond to it (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Legitimate interests — to operate and secure our website and to handle correspondence and recruitment, balanced against your rights (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Steps prior to entering a contract — where you contact us about a partnership, evaluation, supply or role (Art. 6(1)(b)).

6. International Data Transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers are US-based, so information you submit is processed in the United States. If you contact us from the EEA or the UK, your data will be transferred outside your country. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (for example, the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses). You can ask us for more detail at any time.

7. Data Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to handle your request — and, for job applications, to consider you for current and future roles — after which we delete or anonymise them. Hosting logs are retained by our provider for a short period for security and operational purposes.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We limit who can access submissions and follow reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights — EU / UK (GDPR)

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you have the right to: access your personal data; have it corrected or erased; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email info@rewinddynamics.com — we will respond within the timeframes the law requires and will not discriminate against you for exercising a right. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

10. Your Rights — California / United States (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to request access to or deletion of it, to request correction, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not process it for cross-context behavioural advertising. To make a request, email info@rewinddynamics.com. Comparable rights may apply under other US state privacy laws; we honour equivalent requests wherever we can.

11. Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. For the full detail — including the technical data our host logs and the web fonts we load — see our Cookie Policy. If we ever add analytics, we will update both pages and, where required, ask for your consent first.

12. Children

This website is intended for a professional audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Privacy & Terms.

How we handle information submitted through this website, and the terms under which it's provided. Last updated 2026.

NOTE — This is a general template provided for the launch of the website. Please have it reviewed by qualified counsel before relying on it for compliance.

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Rewind Dynamics ("Rewind Dynamics", "we", "us"), a defence technology company based in New Delhi, India. You can reach us at info@rewinddynamics.com for any privacy question.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us through the forms on this site:

  • Contact details you submit (name, email, phone, organization).
  • The content of your message or application, and the enquiry or role type.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends (such as IP address and user agent) as part of normal web operation.

3. How We Use It

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, evaluate applications, and communicate with you about your request. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Form Delivery

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form-relay service. By submitting a form you agree to that processing. Please do not submit sensitive, export-controlled or classified information through this website.

5. Data Retention

We keep submissions only as long as needed to handle your request or, for applications, to consider you for current and future roles — after which we delete or anonymise them.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've submitted. Email info@rewinddynamics.com and we'll action reasonable requests.

7. Cookies & Analytics

This site is intentionally lightweight and does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we'll update this page and, where required, ask for your consent.

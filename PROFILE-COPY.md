## Long description (Crunchbase / directories, ~900 chars)

Rewind Dynamics is a defence-technology company founded on 26 January 2020 and
a global company. We develop the autonomy core behind three
families of systems: unmanned aerial systems (UAV) for intelligence,
surveillance and reconnaissance; precision guidance for accurate, accountable
effects; and tactical miniature platforms for close-range, dismounted use.

Our engineering focus is the software layer — onboard autonomy, perception and
sensor fusion, edge AI, and navigation that survives GPS denial — built to
integrate with existing platforms rather than replace them. A person authorises
every consequential action; we do not build systems that select and engage on
their own authority.

The company is at research-and-development stage. Nothing has been fielded, and
our first end-to-end real-world mission test is planned for December 2026. We
work with governments, armed forces, primes and integrators worldwide, subject
to end-use assessment and export-control screening.

## Where to create profiles, in priority order

1. **LinkedIn Company Page** — linkedin.com/company/setup/new
   Highest-value single link. Google trusts it heavily for company entities.
2. **Crunchbase** — crunchbase.com/register (free "Add a company")
   Feeds many aggregators; strong entity corroboration.
3. **Google Business Profile** — only if you will accept a postcard at the
   Skip street address unless it is a staffed, publishable office.
4. **GitHub organisation** — github.com/organizations/new
   Credible for a software-first company even with one public repo.
5. **X / YouTube** — lower priority; create to protect the handle.

After each one goes live, paste the URL into `tools/social.py` and run it.

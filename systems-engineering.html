Capability 05  ·  Systems Engineering

Systems
engineering.

Clever autonomy that only works in a lab is worth nothing. Systems engineering is the discipline that turns software, sensors, compute and structure into one machine that behaves the same way on a bad day as it did on the bench.

Why It Decides Everything

The unglamorous work
that makes it real.

§ 01 / RATIONALE

Most of the distance between a promising prototype and a dependable system is not algorithmic. It is integration, power, thermal behaviour, vibration, connectors, timing, configuration management and test. These are the things that decide whether a platform works in the field.

We treat this as a first-class engineering discipline rather than an afterthought at the end of development. Building it in from the start is considerably cheaper than discovering it during trials.

What It Covers

From bench
to field.

Systems engineering is the connective tissue: making sure that each subsystem is sound, that the interfaces between them are defined, and that the whole behaves predictably.

  • IntegrationBringing autonomy, sensors, compute, power and structure together as one coherent machine.
  • Reliability & environmentVibration, temperature, dust, moisture and the ordinary abuse of real use.
  • Verification & testEstablishing that the system does what it is specified to do — repeatably, not once.
  • Configuration controlKnowing exactly which software and hardware are in any given unit, and being able to reproduce it.

How We Work

Principles we
hold to.

§ 02 / PRINCIPLES
01

Design for test

If a behaviour cannot be tested repeatably, it cannot be trusted.

02

Fail predictably

Define what happens when something breaks, before it breaks.

03

Record everything

Every run is captured so it can be rewound, reviewed and learned from.

04

Modularity

Clean interfaces so a subsystem can be improved without destabilising the rest.

Everything described here is under active research and development. Nothing has been fielded, and we do not publish specifications or performance figures. Our first end-to-end mission test is planned for December 2026. Technical detail is shared directly with partners, evaluators and the armed forces under appropriate terms — get in touch.

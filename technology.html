How It Works

The technology,
in plain terms.

A short, honest overview of what makes a machine "autonomous" — the pieces involved and how they fit together. Where useful, we've linked out to trusted references and research so you can read further.

The Basic Idea

Sense · Decide · Act.

At its simplest, an autonomous system repeats one loop: take in information about the world, decide what to do, and do it — then repeat, many times a second.

§ 01 / OVERVIEW
STEP 01

Sense

Cameras and sensors gather information about the surroundings.

STEP 02

Understand

That raw data is turned into a usable picture of what's there.

STEP 03

Decide

The system chooses an action based on its goal and the situation.

STEP 04

Act

It moves or responds — then starts the loop again.

How We Work

Test in software first.

A lot of progress happens in simulation — testing ideas in software before committing to hardware. It's faster, safer and cheaper to catch problems on screen than in the field.

  • SimulateTry things in software and repeat them as many times as needed.
  • Test on hardwareMove to real devices once the software is solid.
  • Record & reviewKeep records of every run so we can learn and improve — the idea behind our name.

Responsibility

People stay in charge.

Autonomy should be dependable and accountable. Our view is straightforward: machines can carry out tasks, but people set the rules and stay responsible for how a system is used.

§ 03 / RESPONSIBILITY
01

Human Oversight

Systems are designed so a person can supervise and step in.

02

Safety First

Sensible limits and safe behaviour built in from the start.

03

Security

Good engineering and security practices as a default, not an afterthought.

04

Reviewable

Keeping records so decisions can be understood after the fact.

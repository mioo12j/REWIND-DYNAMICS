How It Works

The autonomy
architecture.

A closed loop that senses the world, reasons about it and acts on it — entirely onboard, continuously improving, and hardened for the environments where it has to work.

The Core Loop

Sense · Reason · Act.

Every autonomous decision passes through the same disciplined loop, thousands of times a second — no round trips, no waiting on the network.

§ 01 / LOOP
STEP 01

Sense

Multi-modal sensors stream a live view of the environment into the perception layer.

STEP 02

Fuse

Signals are fused into one uncertainty-aware world model the system can trust.

STEP 03

Reason

The autonomy core plans and re-plans against mission goals and rules of engagement.

STEP 04

Act

Control executes the plan on the platform — safely, deterministically, in real time.

Architecture

A layered, modular stack.

Each layer is independently testable and replaceable. That modularity is what lets one autonomy stack move across platforms — and lets us improve any layer without rebuilding the whole.

Open interfaces mean the stack integrates with existing platforms, payloads and command-and-control rather than demanding a rip-and-replace.

Development

Simulation-first.
Field-proven.

We fly millions of virtual miles before a body leaves the bench. High-fidelity simulation lets us stress autonomy against edge cases, failures and adversaries that would be impossible — or dangerous — to stage in the real world.

  • Massive-scale simulationAdversarial scenarios, sensor degradation and rare events, replayed at scale.
  • Hardware-in-the-loopReal compute and sensors validated against simulated worlds before field trials.
  • Then the real worldWe field in the conditions that matter and let the data decide.

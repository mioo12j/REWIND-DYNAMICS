Applications

Where autonomy
earns its place.

Our three platform families exist to serve a small number of jobs that genuinely benefit from autonomy — jobs that are dull, dangerous, or simply beyond what a small team can sustain by hand. These are the application areas we build toward.

The Principle

Start with the job,
not the machine.

§ 01 / APPROACH

It is easy to build an impressive platform that answers no particular question. We work the other way round: identify a task where a person is currently exposed to risk, or where attention is the limiting factor, and ask whether autonomy meaningfully changes it.

Every application below shares the same underlying core — autonomy, perception, edge intelligence and dependable engineering. That is deliberate. Improving the core improves every application at once, which is why we invest there rather than in bespoke one-off systems.

§ 03 / COMMON

Everything described here is under active research and development. Nothing has been fielded, and we do not publish specifications or performance figures.

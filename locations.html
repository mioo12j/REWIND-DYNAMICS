One engineering facility, and customers wherever autonomy is needed. We are deliberately concentrated at this stage — the work benefits from everyone being in the same building — while the systems we build are intended for defence and military users worldwide.

Our Footprint

Concentrated by
choice.

§ 01 / FOOTPRINT

Rewind Dynamics runs from a single engineering facility, where software engineering, laboratory and test infrastructure sit together. At our stage that concentration is an advantage rather than a limitation: autonomy is developed by tight iteration between software, hardware and test, and that goes faster when the people doing it share a room.

Working from one place says nothing about who we build for. Our systems are designed for defence and military users internationally, and we engage with programmes, primes and evaluators wherever they are — subject to the export-control and end-user assessment set out in our security & compliance and human rights pages.

Expanding our engineering facility in New Delhi

More space, better test infrastructure and room to grow the team — an update on our expanded New Delhi facility.

Building autonomous systems takes benches, test space and room for a growing team. We're pleased to share that we've expanded our engineering facility in New Delhi.

Built for building

The expanded space gives us more integration and test area, better infrastructure for hardware work, and the room to bring on the engineers we need as our programmes grow. It's a practical milestone rather than a flashy one — but practical milestones are what real capability is made of.

Why New Delhi

We're deliberately building this capability in India, and New Delhi keeps us close to the institutions, talent and ecosystem we work within. Indigenous capability isn't only about where a product is assembled; it's about where the understanding lives.

Sovereign capability is built in the country, by people who stay in the country.

We're hiring

More space means more people. If you want to work on hard autonomy problems from the ground floor, take a look at our open roles.
